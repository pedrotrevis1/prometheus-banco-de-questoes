# Feature specification: Help and legal copy

**Folder**: `015-help-and-legal-copy` | **Status**: Implemented | **Created**: 2026-11-19

## Acceptance scenarios

1. **Given** the Ajuda dialog is open, **then** its instructions describe the
   current menu, backup, question, search and exam workflows.
2. **Given** the Sobre dialog is open, **then** a readable fine-print notice
   explains that the developer does not control or assume responsibility for
   the user's use of the app or user-provided content, subject to applicable law.
3. **Given** either theme, **then** the notice remains readable below the
   donation button.

## Scope

Copy and presentation only. No application behavior, storage, export, or
network behavior changes.
